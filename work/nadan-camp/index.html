Nadan Camp

A travel community’s Instagram, started at zero and grown past 1,700.

Role
Account build, content, paid social
Scope
Content planning, reels, Meta ad campaigns, daily handling
Live
@nadancamp_ ↗
The brief

Nadan Camp runs group travel across India for a Kerala audience. When I started, the account was empty: no followers, no posts, no audience to inherit.

Travel is also one of the harder things to sell cold. Nobody books a trip with strangers from a single advert. They need to see where the group actually goes, and what being on one of those trips looks like, before a booking message feels reasonable.

What I did

Built the account from zero

Set up the page, its identity and its voice, and established a posting rhythm from an empty start.

Reels-first content

Travel is a moving-image subject, so the content leads with reels from real trips: Wayanad, Manali, Ladakh, Rajasthan and the Hornbill festival in Nagaland.

Campaign creatives

Designed trip-specific posts carrying the dates and a direct WhatsApp call to action, so interest converts into a booking conversation.

Meta ad campaigns

Paid promotion put behind the reels that earned it organically, rather than boosting everything evenly.

Ongoing handling

Planning, posting and running the campaigns as a continuing job.

Where it landed

From an empty account to past 1,700 followers, with a content and paid system running behind it: reels prove the trips are real, and campaign posts turn that into booking conversations.

05 / FAQ

Questions I get asked.

The practical stuff: what I do, where I work, and how the results get measured.

What does Muhammed KV do as a digital marketer and web developer?

I cover both sides of an online presence. On the development side I build fast, mobile-first websites and e-commerce stores on clean, SEO-ready structure. On the marketing side I run SEO, Meta Ads, Google Ads, social media handling and content. The site that gets built also gets found.

Where are you based, and do you work with clients outside Calicut?

I'm based in Calicut, Kerala, and I work with businesses across Kerala and the rest of India. Design reviews, campaign changes and reporting all happen online, so distance isn't a limitation. I work remotely as readily as locally.

Do you handle the website and the marketing together?

Yes, and that's usually the point. A site built without search in mind is expensive to fix later, and ads pointed at a slow page waste budget. Building the site and running the campaigns together means the structure, the copy and the landing pages line up from day one.

What kind of projects have you worked on?

Three stand out. Zedutoys is an educational-toys e-commerce site where I contributed to the design and development and applied branding throughout. Royal Trading Academy is a UAE forex institute whose Instagram posts I design and whose page I handle day to day. Nadan Camp is a Kerala travel community whose Instagram I started from zero and now run, ads included. Alongside those I've done the UI/UX, banner design and product design for seven live e-commerce storefronts across the UAE, Saudi Arabia and India. I'm currently at Hello Aura, working across web design, graphic design, branding and digital marketing for client businesses.

How do you measure marketing results?

Tracking gets set up before a campaign runs: conversions, form fills, purchases, whichever action actually matters to the business. Reporting is tied to those numbers rather than reach or impressions alone, so you can see what the spend returned.

What's your background?

A B.Sc. in Chemistry from the University of Calicut, plus digital marketing training at Fragmtree Campus, Calicut. The science background is where the analytical half comes from. I treat campaigns as tests with measurable outcomes rather than guesses.

How long does a website take to build?

It depends on scope. A focused landing page is quick; an e-commerce store with a catalogue, payments and content takes longer. I'll give you a realistic timeline once we've talked through what the site actually needs to do.
